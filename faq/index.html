A Notary Public is a legal practitioner appointed by the Chief Justice of Nigeria and authorised to prepare and authenticate documents for use in Nigeria and abroad, as well as to administer and commission oaths, certify copies of documents and verify addresses. You need a Notary for affidavits, powers of attorney, statutory declarations, and any document that will be relied upon by a foreign authority or institution.

Yes. The Notaries Public Act 2023 provides for digital and virtual notary sessions through Remote Online Notarisation (RON).
