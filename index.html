Our Heritage & Mandate

A decade of trusted advocacy since 2016 — elevated with full Notarial authority.

Originally established in 2016, the practice built a distinguished track record representing corporate institutions, property investors, employers, families, and litigants across Oyo State and Nigeria’s commercial centres.

In 2025, following the appointment and swearing-in of Principal Barnabas O. Atungwu, Esq. as a Notary Public of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the firm formally registered its expanded practice name as Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors and Notary Public — uniting courtroom advocacy, Company Secretarial governance, and internationally recognised Notarial certification under one roof.

2016 · Foundation Established in Ibadan as a full-service commercial, property, employment & dispute resolution practice.
2025 · Notarial Elevation Registered as Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors & Notary Public upon appointment as Notary Public for Nigeria.

Core Practice Desks

Comprehensive legal, company secretarial & notarial capability.

Tailored advisory, regulatory mastery, and resolute advocacy across eight specialized desks.

01 / NOTARIAL AUTHORITY

Notary Public

Statutory notarisation, witnessing, and authentication of documents for local and international use — affidavits, declarations, powers of attorney, embassy, academic, and cross-border trade instruments.

02 / GOVERNANCE & CAC

Company Secretarial Practitioners

Retained Company Secretarial services, board & AGM governance, statutory registers, CAC incorporations, post-incorporation filings, restructuring, and CAMA compliance audits.

03 / POWER & UTILITIES

Electricity Law

Advisory under the Electricity Act & State Electricity Markets — embedded generation, mini-grid & distribution licensing, Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), and regulatory compliance.

04 / MATRIMONIAL & ESTATE

Family Law and Divorce

Discreet, compassionate representation in matrimonial causes, child custody, property settlement, adoption, wills, letters of administration, and probate.

05 / PROPERTY & LAND

Real Estate & Title Perfection

Thorough land registry due diligence across Oyo, Lagos, Abuja & Plateau, perfection of title, deeds of assignment, commercial leases, and property management advisory.

06 / COMMERCIAL TRANSACTIONS

Corporate & Commercial Law

Commercial contract drafting, joint ventures, employment & labour law advisory before the National Industrial Court, debt recovery, and transaction structuring.

07 / ADVOCACY & ADR

Litigation & Dispute Resolution

Decisive representation in civil, commercial, property, human rights, and employment disputes across trial and appellate courts, alongside mediation and arbitration.

08 / SECTOR SPECIALISM

Medical & Healthcare Law

Regulatory compliance, clinical governance audits, medico-legal risk management, and legal representation for private hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals.
