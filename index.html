THE FIRM & HERITAGE

A DECADE OF ADVOCACY,
ELEVATED BY NOTARIAL SEAL.

Originally established in 2016, our practice has spent a decade advising corporate boards, commercial enterprises, real estate investors, power-sector stakeholders, families, and litigants with disciplined legal judgment and quiet discretion.

In 2025, following the appointment and swearing-in of Principal Barnabas O. Atungwu, Esq. as a Notary Public of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the firm formally registered its expanded practice name as Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors and Notary Public — seamlessly integrating courtroom advocacy, retained Company Secretarial governance, and internationally recognised Notarial authentication.

THE PRACTICE

EIGHT SPECIALIZED DESKS,
CLEARLY DEFINED.

Select any practice desk below to initiate a structured brief directly with Chambers.

01

Notary Public & Document Authentication

Statutory notarisation, witnessing, and certification of affidavits, declarations, powers of attorney, academic credentials, immigration files, and cross-border commercial instruments.

02

Company Secretarial Practitioners

Retained board & AGM governance, statutory registers, CAC incorporations, post-incorporation filings, annual returns, share restructuring, and CAMA 2020 compliance audits.

03

Electricity & Energy Law

Regulatory and transactional advisory under the Electricity Act & State Electricity Markets — embedded generation, mini-grid licensing, Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), and tariff disputes.

04

Family Law, Matrimonial Causes & Probate

Discreet representation in divorce & matrimonial proceedings, child custody, property settlement, adoption, wills, letters of administration, and estate probate.

05

Real Estate & Land Title Perfection

Comprehensive land registry due diligence across Oyo, Lagos, Abuja & Plateau, perfection of title, deeds of assignment, commercial leases, and Diaspora property acquisition safeguards.

06

Corporate, Commercial & Employment Law

Commercial contract structuring, joint ventures, debt recovery, and executive employment & labour advisory before the National Industrial Court of Nigeria.

07

Litigation, Arbitration & Mediation

Resolute advocacy across trial and appellate courts in civil, commercial, constitutional, and property disputes, paired with structured commercial mediation and arbitration.

08

Medical & Healthcare Regulatory Law

Clinical governance, statutory licensing compliance, medico-legal risk management, and institutional representation for hospitals, diagnostic centres, and practitioners.
