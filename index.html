About Chambers

A nationwide legal hub built on precision, confidentiality, and integrity.

Registered in 2025 under its expanded practice mandate as Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors and Notary Public, the firm operates from its Head Office at Law Forte Complex, Ibadan, with strategic correspondence offices in Abuja, Lagos (Lekki Phase 1), and Jos.

Operating as Legal Practitioners, Notary Public, and Company Secretarial Practitioners, we advise corporate boards, power & electricity sector operators, property investors, healthcare institutions, families, and Nigerians in the Diaspora with proactive, transparent, and results-driven counsel.
