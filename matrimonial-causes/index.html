Matrimonial Causes · First Contact

Start with a conflict check.

This is a brief first-contact enquiry for divorce or dissolution, judicial separation, annulment or a related matrimonial matter. The firm will check for conflicts before asking for detailed instructions.

There is no charge to send this enquiry.

This form asks only for the names needed for an initial conflict check, a broad matter type and a safe way to contact you. Do not include your account of what happened, information about children, property or finances, identity numbers, or documents.

Your answers stay on your device until you tap the send button, and nothing is sent before that. When you tap send, the page submits the enquiry over an encrypted connection to FormSubmit, a form delivery service, which forwards it by email to the firm's mailbox. The website itself does not store your answers. No solicitor and client relationship is created unless the firm confirms it can act and accepts instructions. This form is not an emergency service.

Fields marked * are mandatory. The enquiry cannot be sent until each one is completed.

1 Names for the conflict check

Please use the full names by which the parties are known. Do not add case facts here.

2 General type of matter

Choose the closest option. No facts or explanation are needed now.

3 A safe way to contact you

Choose the method and details the firm may safely use. If it is not safe to contact you, do not submit contact details here; contact the firm when it is safe for you.

4 Your confirmation

Authentication of information. By sending this preliminary enquiry, I confirm the names and contact details I supplied are true, correct and authentic to the best of my knowledge.

Your enquiry is delivered to the firm's mailbox. Do not include case facts or documents at this stage.
