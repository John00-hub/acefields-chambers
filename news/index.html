Service Announcement

Remote Online Notarisation now offered to clients at home and abroad

Following the Notaries Public Act 2023, the firm now conduct virtual notary sessions. Clients in the diaspora no longer need to travel to Nigeria to have an affidavit, power of attorney or statutory declaration notarised. Identity is verified during the session, and each notarisation is recorded in keeping with the Act.

Principal Counsel elevated to the office of Notary Public for the Federal Republic of Nigeria

The firm record the conferment of the Notarial seal, following which the practice was re-registered in 2025 as Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors and Notary Public. Notarial services are now available to clients in Ibadan, Abuja, Lagos and Jos.

Practice Notice

Documents intended for use abroad require notarisation in person

Clients are reminded that notarisation is performed in the presence of the Notary. Scanned copies alone cannot be notarised. Original documents and a valid means of identification must be presented.
