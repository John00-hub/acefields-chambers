What to bring to the appointment

Bring all of the following. A missing item is the only reason a notarial appointment fails on the day.

  • The original document to be notarised, unsigned and undated
  • A valid means of identification with photograph: passport, driver’s licence or national identity card
  • Proof of address: a utility bill or bank statement issued in the last three months
  • A passport photograph, where the receiving authority requires one
  • For a company document: the certificate of incorporation and proof that the signatory is a director or the secretary
  • The checklist or instructions of the authority receiving the document, where it is going abroad

How the appointment runs

You appear in person, because a notary must see the signatory and confirm identity. Identification is checked against the document, the document is executed in the notary’s presence, and the notarial act is completed and sealed. A single document is usually finished in under an hour.

Where the document is to be used outside Nigeria, the receiving authority often requires authentication beyond the notary’s seal. That step runs through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and adds two to four weeks. Tell the firm where the document is going, and the correct route is confirmed before you attend.

Documents the firm notarises

Powers of attorney, affidavits and statutory declarations, deeds of assignment, consent letters, bank and company documents, academic and professional certificates, adoption and immigration papers, and certified true copies of originals. Where the document does not yet exist, the firm drafts it and notarises it at the same appointment.
