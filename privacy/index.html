Data Protection

Privacy policy for this website.

This policy explains what personal information Acefields Attorneys, Solicitors and Notary Public collects through this website, why it is collected, how long it is held, and how a client may ask for it to be removed.

Last updated 7 November 2026

2. The most important thing to understand

This website does not store the information a client types into any form. There is no database behind these pages.

When a client fills in a registration form, the details stay in the page on the client's own device until the client taps the send button. Nothing is transmitted before that. On sending, the page assembles a single message, adds the photographs, signatures and identity documents the client has attached, and submits it over an encrypted connection to FormSubmit, a form delivery service, which forwards it by email to the firm's mailbox.

The client can review everything before sending, and can therefore choose to leave the page without sending, in which case nothing reaches the firm and nothing is kept.

A matrimonial enquiry works in the same way. The answers stay on the device until the client taps send, and are then delivered to the firm by email through FormSubmit. The firm does not ask for a case narrative, documents, identity numbers or details about children before it has checked that it can act.

If a client fills in a form, closes the page and does not send, nothing reaches the firm and nothing is retained by the website. The information simply disappears with the page.

3. What the firm receives

The firm only receives what a client chooses to send, and only after the client sends it. In practice that is:

Through the registration pages

Business Name, Company and Incorporated Trustees applications may include:

  • Name, residential address, date of birth, gender, nationality and occupation of proprietors, directors, shareholders or trustees
  • Telephone number and email address
  • National Identification Number (NIN)
  • Proposed business names, objects of the company or objects of the organisation
  • Business address, email address and telephone number
  • Passport photographs, specimen signatures and identity cards, which the client attaches to the form and which are transmitted with the submission

This information is necessary to prepare and file an application at the Corporate Affairs Commission. The firm cannot lodge the application without it.

Through the enquiry and template request forms

  • Name, telephone number and, where given, email address
  • The nature of the enquiry, and any details a client chooses to describe
  • For template requests, the template selected and any order reference

Matrimonial enquiries

A matrimonial enquiry reaches the firm through the enquiry form on the contact page, in the same way as any other enquiry. The firm asks for detailed instructions, and for the information a matrimonial matter requires, only after it has checked that it can act.

If you are in immediate danger, contact the police first. The website enquiry form is not monitored continuously and is not an emergency service.

6. Where conversations live

Every form on this site that collects details, including the registration forms and the enquiry form, delivers them the same way: the browser submits them over an encrypted connection to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails them, with any attachments, to the firm's mailbox.

FormSubmit therefore handles the information in transit. Its published documentation states that submissions are kept in its archive for 30 days, and that uploaded files are not kept in that archive. The firm receives a copy in its own mailbox, which it controls, and holds that copy as part of its client records.

The firm has not yet obtained a written data processing agreement from FormSubmit. Until that is in place, the firm limits what it asks for at first contact, as described below. If a client would rather not use this route at all, they may write to info@acefieldsattorneys.com.ng or telephone the firm and give the details another way.

Detailed instructions and documents for a matrimonial matter are requested only after the initial conflict check, through a method the firm confirms with the prospective client.

7. Visitors to the website

This website does not use cookies for advertising and does not track visitors from page to page. No consent banner is needed because no personal profile of a visitor is built.

The firm measures how many people visit, which pages are opened, and the country from which they come, using Cloudflare Web Analytics. That service produces aggregate counts only. It does not use cookies, does not build a profile of a visitor, and does not identify any individual.

8. How long information is kept

  • Registrations and transaction files: for as long as the firm is required to keep them, and in any event while the firm holds itself available to the client in respect of the file.
  • Enquiries that do not become instructions: held only while the enquiry is being handled, and then removed.
  • Template requests: held only for as long as is needed to prepare and deliver the document and account for payment.
  • Matrimonial files: for as long as the matter is running, and afterwards for as long as the firm is required to keep a client file. Where a court order concerns a child, the file may need to be kept until the child reaches adulthood.
  • Matrimonial enquiries that do not become instructions: held only while the enquiry is being handled, and then removed. Such an enquiry is never added to a mailing list and is never referred to elsewhere.

A client may ask for information held about them to be deleted at any time. See section 10.

9. How the information is protected

Access is limited to the person handling the matter. Documents are kept in controlled locations and are not left where they may be read by others. The website itself carries no database of client information, which means there is nothing stored behind these pages for an attacker to reach.

10. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, a client may ask the firm:

  • To confirm what personal information the firm holds about them
  • To correct anything that is wrong
  • To delete information that is no longer needed
  • To restrict what the firm does with it
  • To provide a copy in a form they can keep
  • To explain how their information has been handled

To make a request, write to info@acefieldsattorneys.com.ng or send a message on WhatsApp to +234 817 991 1555. The firm will respond without undue delay.

A person who is not satisfied with the firm's response may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

11. Children

This website is not directed at children, and the firm does not knowingly collect information from a child through it. Where a matter concerns a minor, the information is provided by a parent or guardian.

12. Changes to this policy

If the way the firm handles information changes, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change. Clients who have an open matter with the firm will be told directly where a change affects them.
