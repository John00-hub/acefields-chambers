CLIENT RESOURCES

Practical guides for common transactions.

Prepared by the chambers so that clients arrive informed, with the right documents in hand.

Plain guidance prepared by the chambers for clients handling common transactions. Each note sets out the documents to gather and the checks to complete before money changes hands.

RON
Remote Online Notarisation: notarise from anywhere in the world

Under the Notaries Public Act 2023, the chambers can notarise documents in a secure virtual session, with no in-person meeting required. This serves clients in the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, the Gulf and across Africa who need Nigerian notarisation without travelling. Affidavits, powers of attorney, statutory declarations, certified copies and address verification can all be completed remotely, subject to identity verification. Contact the chambers to arrange a session.

01
Property purchase due diligence checklist

The documents to obtain before paying for land or a building, the checks to run at the land registry, and the red flags that should stop a transaction. Covers title documents, the survey plan, the deed of assignment and confirmation that the land is free of government acquisition.

02
Notarisation: what to bring

A short guide to notarial appointments. The original document, a valid means of identification and proof of address. The name on your identification must match the name on the document. The person signing must appear in person.

03
Powers of attorney and statutory declarations

When a power of attorney is required, who may execute it, and the circumstances in which a statutory declaration must be sworn before a Notary or Commissioner for Oaths.

04
Company registration and company secretarial filings

What the Corporate Affairs Commission requires for incorporation, the documents needed for an annual return, and the records a company must keep at its registered office. In the chambers' experience a Business Name takes 1 hour to 3 days, a Private Limited Liability Company 1 hour to 7 working days, and Incorporated Trustees from 40 days inclusive of the Registrar General's Consent.

05
Preparing a brief for counsel

How to set out a matter so that counsel can advise quickly: the parties, the facts in date order, the documents relied on, what has already been done, and what outcome you are seeking.

06
Employment: offer letters, contracts and termination

The terms a written contract of employment must contain, notice periods, the treatment of accrued entitlements, and the records an employer should retain.

Please note. These notes are general guidance and are not legal advice on any particular matter. Every transaction turns on its own facts. Please contact the chambers before acting on any of the points above.
